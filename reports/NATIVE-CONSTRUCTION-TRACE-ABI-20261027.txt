The seven entries use aligned eight-byte CAS ownership. The two callsites use

No page may become writable after any entry can execute from it.

The FloatState prefix subtracts 0x38 from RSP and saves XMM6 at RSP+0x20. It needs
prefix-only unwind metadata and a continuation that RtlVirtualUnwind will not
misclassify as an epilogue. The complete pinned 120-byte function has no R11
register access, independently checked instruction by instruction; an audited
MOV R11/indirect-JMP continuation is therefore a candidate. Twelve metadata bytes
fit at slot+48. Registration must validate the sealed bytes and occur before CAS,
remain resident across STOP/failed installation, and be tested at each instruction
boundary and continuation with nonvolatile XMM state. The other six prefixes do
not change RSP or a nonvolatile register value. The shared unwind implementation
is separately reviewed; this contract alone does not implement it.
