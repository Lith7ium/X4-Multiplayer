SELECTED POSITION RNG: STRICT STREAM DISCRIMINATION REVIEW FIX
28 October 2026

Independent review of frozen afe9f899 found that the raw Position reader's
new exact-prefix filter silently ignored malformed family members. A valid
natural fixture followed by WORLD_POSITION_BOGUS, bare WORLD_POSITION, or
bare WORLD_POSITION_STOP could retain selected_valid=true. The previous raw
reader rejected those records. This is a parser regression, not native evidence.

The follow-up restores broad WORLD_POSITION family screening. Only the exact
WORLD_POSITION_RNG and WORLD_POSITION_RNG_STOP record names are delegated to
their dedicated strict reader, and only when an explicit intervention context
is supplied. Natural-only parsing rejects those intervention records. Unknown
suffixes and bare raw Position records remain failures in both modes. Bare
recognized RNG records are delegated and then rejected by the RNG reader; they
cannot authorize the raw-to-intervention bridge.

Tests now feed the combined Position and RNG stream to both readers. They cover
the three review witnesses, unknown RNG suffixes, unconfigured intervention
rows, malformed recognized RNG rows, and claimed paired success refusal. This
preserves the distinction between validity of a selected intervention receipt
and failure of the broader raw capture: a malformed unrelated raw row cannot
make the raw capture or a claimed paired session pass.

Validation: 73 focused Python/Lua tests pass. Existing actual C formatter
exports still pass the readers: 122 RNG bridge mutants and 225 raw Position
mutants are rejected, including four coherent class differences and the raw
untrimmed-row bound. These are owned-memory exports with modeled completed
parent/Value/Find/Wait dependencies, not game captures. No C/header, hook,
thunk, RNG behavior, configuration, game artifact or old raw log is changed.
The frozen implementation remains afe9f899; this is a separate review fix.

Fresh import-only replay with this correction retains World014's exact 64
