Exception proposal, approved separately for source-only implementation

Origin post/delivery and outer admission need __try/__finally with no __except.
On abnormal termination, clean only own TLS/admission once, record separate unwind
counters/error, and propagate the original exception. Do not count a normal return,
set delivered-state2, capture after-state, or read the transferred event. Equivalent
cleanup is needed for transfer/publication/activation, including excluded-scope
shadow restoration. Never hold a lock across original. Normal results stay unchanged.
The schema must distinguish ordinary returns from unwinds explicitly.

Queue enqueue/dispatch owns additional transition admission, TLS and pending-ticket
lifetime. Its abnormal callback/cleanup is a separate World-owned dependency.
Origin plus the three direct transition wrappers do not prove full transition
exception cleanup. Tests must use valid caller unwind metadata and actual adapter
paths: the rotation agent demonstrated that a fabricated caller without pdata can
skip an outer finally despite a language-only RaiseException probe passing. Require
propagation, nested selected/excluded scopes, STOP during unwind, original once,
and no post-abnormal native reads. No exception changes are in this consumer commit.
