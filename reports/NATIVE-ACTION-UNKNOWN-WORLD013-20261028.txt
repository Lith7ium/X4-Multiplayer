Result
------

NPC -> Cockpit -> ShipMedium -> saved Zone 0x3fd chain. The first three objects

What is and is not established
-----------------------------
The three unmapped objects are outside the complete saved import identity set.
They are not evidence of an omitted saved NPC/cockpit/medium-ship class, and
their current full-ID registry checks do not show a stale owner pointer.
The supported saved ancestor is real and portable; its children are not yet
portable. Creation or reconstruction during/after loading is a plausible
explanation, but no receipt records the relevant birth or replacement event.
Unsaved/generated or surviving native state cannot be distinguished here.
Numeric searches for runtime IDs inside the save cannot answer that question.

A future portable identity would require a proved creation/import transition
and its causal owner/order, not a name, local ID, class, Zone or script tuple.
The existing receipt is sufficient to choose a bounded forensic investigation,
not to assign that identity, seed its RNG, suppress reason 6 or enroll the chain.
No new interception or canonical-ID design is proposed by this report.
