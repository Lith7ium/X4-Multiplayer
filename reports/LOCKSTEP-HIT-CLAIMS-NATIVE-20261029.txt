LOCKSTEP PILOT FIRING AND HIT CLAIMS: NATIVE SIDE
29 October 2026 | static analysis + offline implementation; no X4 launched, no live result yet

SUMMARY

Follows reports/LOCKSTEP-PREDICTION-20261029.txt (prediction, claim design and
the pure claim/container code). Implemented on contrib/ferrite-lockstep-
prediction, rebased onto the coordinator head 6075f51e, all default off:
- Pilot firing (native_gate_pilot_fire, stop reason 112): the record's trigger
  bit fires the slot ship's primary guns at the apply tick on every client
  through the native weapon code. Data writes only (a one-byte trigger per
  gun) plus two native calls per gun at lease start; no code patch, no thunk.
- Hit claims, native side (native_gate_hit_claims, stop reason 111): portable
  projectile keys, deterministic suppression of native hits involving
  predicted slot ships, the owner's ahead evaluation (incoming against the
  displayed pose, outgoing shifted d ticks), claim emission into the hits
  section, admission, resolution and native injection (OnHit + kill) with a
  keyed RNG word. One call-site redirect of the nearest-hit ray cast through
  a private stub page; no thunk recipe (count stays 55), no entry patch.
- Lua surface: gate.hit_claims_admit/resolve/proposal for the 'hits'
  section of the probe's input multiplexer (section C; merged with the
  multiplexer and the protocol 4 lookahead), gate.input_mux/input_split as
  the C reference of the container.
Static facts below come from two RE passes (firing; hit pipeline) checked
against the binary with capstone and SHA-256 pins; every pin is verified
against X4.exe 9.00 in the native-image test.

A. PILOT FIRING (lockstep_pilot_fire.h)

Native mechanism (RE, high unless noted). Every weapon owns its fire decision
through its shoot controller (weapon+0x318). Weapon::Move 0x8132d0 runs
UpdateShootDecision 0x816d20, then fires while the next shot is due and
CanFire allows (Fire 0x817d20 -> FireShot 0x8185b0 per barrel and salvo),
handles heat, start effects and the firing byte +0x30d, then aims. Controller
slot 14 (+0x70) "wants to fire", slot 28 (+0xe0) "fire without target". The
MindlessShootController (vtable 0x2b7cf68) is what the stock <shoot> action
installs: slot 14 = trigger byte +0xa0 != 0 (0x7e58a0), slot 28 true, no
target, so the decision skips angle, range and line of sight; gimbals go to
rest (StopAiming). Its only writers are slot 31 (0x7e58b0, from start/stop
shooting) and the save loader; nothing rewrites it per frame.
Gun selection: the ship's active primary weapon group (ship+0x638 vector of
0x18-byte UniverseID vectors, active index +0x668), components of exactly
U::Weapon 0x2b68278 under the ship (turrets 0x2b6e910, missile launchers
0x2b02988 and other ships' guns excluded), group order.
Lease (first tick >= window start, path_release, every client, slot order):
- a gun with no controller: 0x819480(gun) (start shooting: installs Mindless,
  sets the trigger, posts ShootTargetSetEvent / WeaponShootTargetSetEvent);
- a gun with another controller (AI Basic, the parked player's
  PlayerShootController): 0x819680(gun) first (stop shooting: on Mindless it
  only clears the trigger, any other controller type is deleted);
- if any gun of the slot is firing (+0x30d) the whole slot waits a tick;
- then the trigger is cleared; the controller pointer is remembered.
These calls allocate and post events on every client at the same point.
Per tick t (path_release, after the pilot frame table of t is published,
before the pathing kick and slot 3): trigger = record bit 0 and not the
record's travel bit (mode 'record'), or a fixed schedule (mode 'script', 30 of
every 120 ticks, offset per slot). Before the trigger is written each leased
gun is checked: still found by its runtime id (else destroyed: dropped,
guns_lost), controller still ours (pointer and Mindless vtable), trigger byte
still what we wrote. Who changed it decides:
- the simulation, on every client at the same tick. vps-H3k2 (remote relay,
  k=2, d=4) stopped both clients at tick ~466 with 112 because slot 2 is an
  AI-flown fixture whose Attack order keeps its Steering alive: its fight
  script (fight.attack.object.fighter) issues <shoot_at> (0x10a8460 ->
  0x81ac60: a new LargeTarget/Multiple/Basic controller on every weapon) and
  <stop_shooting>/<shoot> (0xbd1e90 -> 0x819680/0x819480: on our Mindless they
  clear or set the trigger); the weapon-mode update 0x4ef380 (Defensible
  vfunc 674) also retargets through 0x81ac60. Such a gun is re-taken at that
  path_release (stop shooting deletes the controller, start shooting installs
  a Mindless; a gun that is firing waits, retake_waits) and a changed trigger
  is rewritten from the record (trigger_restored); counted, not fatal.
- possibly this client alone: any change on the local player's ship (its
  weapon-group and fire keys act there) and any player controller class on
  any ship (PlayerShootController 0x2b7d1a8, PlayerBombLauncherShootController
  0x2b7d2c8, each pinned by vtable slots only it uses; an unreadable
  controller counts as local). These stop the gate with reason 112, stage
  controller_replaced.
The status names the last replacement (last_replaced=t/slot/gun/class/
local|sim). Weapon::Move in slot 3 of tick t then fires.
Window end clears the triggers once (a gun the simulation took meanwhile is
let go, let_go); every stop clears only triggers that are still ours.
Receipt PFIRE_1:tick:firing:fold per tick (slot 6): every leased gun's
firing byte, last-shot time +0x310 and magazine +0x350, folded with
lockstep_seed, appended to the declared state.
Requirements: piloting prototype armed (any source), native_gate_actor_rng_all
= true (the launch, spread and lifetime draws of FireShot 0x8186b2 and
BulletSetup 0xd5103a/0xd5106d/0xd51581/0xd5187f run in the gun's Move scope,
keyed per ship), native_gate_bullet_cadence = 'every' (BulletSetup calls
UpdateFlight at creation; its camera-dependent draw at 0xd50435 and next-test
time are what the 'every' rewrite removes), world identities.
Pins: Weapon::Move 0x8132d0 (5713), shoot decision 0x816d20 (4047), start
shooting 0x819480 (0x1f7), stop shooting 0x819680 (0x25c, contiguous extent),
Mindless leaves 0x7e58a0 (11) / 0x7e58b0 (9), Mindless vtable slots +0x70,
+0x88, +0xe0, +0xf8.
Not covered / risks: the engine refuses to fire for the LOCAL player's ship
during travel drive charging/active and in highway zones unless the local
player is in that zone (0x6d8830/0x6d8870, zone vfunc928): client-local
branches; the travel bit mask covers the first only for records; in the
sandbox both clients sit in the same ship, so they agree. Gimballed guns that
were aimed before the lease fire off-axis until they settle (lease at window
start). Charged weapons never fire without a target. Missiles, turrets and
beams are out of scope. Restoring the player's PlayerShootController at the
end is not done (weapon-group activation reinstalls it). On an AI-flown slot
ship the fight script and the pilot share the guns: between the script's
<shoot_at> and the next path_release the AI controller drives the gun (on
every client alike), and each re-take posts the stock shoot events again. A
client-local UI change of a non-occupied slot ship's weapon modes would be
taken for the simulation; such a UI change diverges the world by itself.

B. HIT CLAIMS, NATIVE SIDE (lockstep_hit_native.h)

Hook. U::Bullet::UpdateFlight 0xd4f240 keeps the bullet in R14 for the whole
function (mov r14, rcx at 0xd4f292, no other write). Its nearest-hit ray cast
call 0xd4fc61 (e8 -> 0xbc4d0) inside the pinned 16-byte block 0xd4fc60 is
redirected (one 16-byte CAS) to a stub on a private page near the image:
  mov [rip -> data+0], r14 ; jmp [rip -> data+8 = hitn_cast]
(code page RX, data page RW). hitn_cast reads the bullet from the slot (only
on the MoveWorker; other threads and late calls after a stop pass through),
runs the original cast exactly once with the original arguments, then works
on its result. Cast ABI (call site + entry): 0xbc4d0 takes fifteen
arguments - system, result, origin float4, ray float4, then eleven stack
slots a5..a15 (entry+0x28..+0x78: an object, 0, 0, byte 0, an unset slot,
a qword, byte 1, three dwords, byte 1, as UpdateFlight stores them at
0xd4fc13..0xd4fc4a) that it hands on to System_CastRay 0xbb5d0. hitn_cast
forwards all fifteen unchanged, to the original and to the owner's extra
casts. (The first build forwarded only eight; System_CastRay then indexed
with stack garbage and both H1 clients faulted at 0xbb6ba, tick ~15.)
Result +0x00 point, +0x10 normal,
+0x20 fraction f32 (-1 none), +0x28 hit component; UpdateFlight takes
fraction >= 0 with a component as the hit, moves the point onto the shield
sphere if shields are up, expresses point and normal in the target's frame
(target vfunc +0x1bb0 at the hit time) and calls OnHit 0xd50500(bullet,
target, hit pose, normal, now), then hit count +0x280 += 1, the ricochet draw
(Random::FloatState) and, without ricochet, event cancel 0x99aa90(bullet,
0xeb) and Kill (vtable +0x1548 = 0x3e51b0, reason 2, killer, now in XMM3).
Piercing bullets (hit limit > 1) use the all-hits cast 0xd4f766 (an RB-tree
of hits); that path is left native in v1.
Identity. A projectile gets its key at its first nearest cast (BulletSetup
casts once in the creation frame; walk order is deterministic):
hit_projectile_key(tick, shooter portable ID, 0, shooter's ordinal of first
casts in that tick), shooter = the portable ID (saved or created) of
bullet+0x2a0 (the weapon). Beams (defaults +0x4a0), piercing bullets
(+0x354 > 1) and shooters without a portable ID get no key and stay native.
Defaults come from GetBulletDefaults 0xe645b0 (pinned lookup by macro).
Suppression (every client). A keyed projectile's native hit is blanked when
the hit component lies under a resolved slot ship (incoming), or the shooter
lies under a slot ship and the hit component lies under a ship with a pathing
read bank (outgoing to a claimable ship). Hits on stations, asteroids and
other non-claimable objects stay native. A blanked segment passes through
the slot ship and anything behind it within that one segment (v1).
Owner evaluation (this client, its own slot ship, once per projectile):
- incoming: the projectile's segment is moved from the displayed pose D of
  the previous tick (lockstep_pilot_predict.h; identity without prediction)
  into the body pose S = the ship's read bank (the pose before this tick's
  join, assumed to be the physics body's pose during the walk) and cast again;
  a hit on the own ship is a claim, point in the ship's frame;
- outgoing: the own projectile's segment is shifted by d ticks of its own
  flight (the controller's velocity, LinearVelocityController +0x80) and cast
  again against the world as it is; a hit on another ship with a read bank and
  a portable ID is a claim, point in that ship's frame (read bank).
The extra casts are read-only queries in UpdateFlight's own context (same
thread and locks); that they have no side effect is assumed, not proven.
Claims and timing: up to 8 per tick are proposed with report t (observe = t)
in the hits section; admitted at t+1 on every client; incoming apply at t+1,
outgoing at t+d (lockstep_hit_claims.h rules). Resolution in slot 6, after the
admission of every slot and before this tick's capture, in (policy rank,
observe tick, slot, index) order with the validation of
lockstep_hit_claims.h on live engine state: projectile found again through
the registry (Find), not a beam/piercer, hit count below its limit, its
position extrapolated as the controller does (start + v (now - t0)); target
found from its portable ID with a read bank in the projectile's zone, rows in
Q30, speed bound from the bank's velocity magnitude, radius bound 1.5 km.
Injection (every client, same order): hit pose = claimed point with identity
rows, normal = against the projectile's flight in the target's frame; the
MoveWorker RNG word is set to lockstep_seed(HIT_DOMAIN, tick, key, target,
slot, "INJ") and restored afterwards (also on unwind), then OnHit, hit count
+1, event cancel, Kill (claimed hits never ricochet). Results fold into the
HITS_1:tick:n:receipt row (plus per-claim slot/key/target/result) appended to
the declared state; a refused claim (consumed, projectile gone, outside the
bounds, policy) is refused identically everywhere.
Stop reason 111: the call block, the stub or its handler changed (checked
every gate tick), or an injection unwound. Restore puts the block back by CAS
(a foreign value is left); the page stays allocated so a call already past
the redirect returns through the pass-through handler.
Pins: call block 0xd4fc60 (16), UpdateFlight prologue 0xd4f240 (0x60), cast
0xbc4d0 (0x7c), OnHit 0xd50500 (0x179), cancel 0x99aa90 (0x84), defaults
0xe645b0 (0xc0), Kill 0x3e51b0 (0x87) and its vtable slot, Find 0xced70.
Refused when x4mp_combat.dll is loaded (it redirects the same call site).
Decision (user, 29 Oct 2026): in player-versus-player hits the player
being hit decides ('victim'); the play config sets
native_gate_hit_claims = 'victim' (overlay-Play16). Policy: shooter-decides by default (configurable 'victim' / 'first'); the
user decision is pending.
Known gaps (v1): piercing and beam projectiles, missiles, rams and area
damage stay native; non-ship targets stay native; the remainder of a blanked
segment is not re-cast; the shield-sphere adjustment of the native path is not
reproduced for claims (ApplyHit applies shields from the target's state); the
UpdateFlight "valid" flag ([rbp+0x1f8]) before the native kill is not checked
for injected kills; presentation of the player's own projectiles d ticks
ahead is not implemented (the outgoing decision uses that geometry, the
player still sees the simulated bullets).

C. INPUT MULTIPLEXER (merged with the coordinator's fa76683a / 112bd45d)

The blob is packed and split by the probe's input multiplexer
(tools/dev/native_gate_inputs_core.lua, the Lua mirror of the container in
lockstep_hit_claims.h; reports/LOCKSTEP-INPUT-MUX-20261029.txt).
gate.input_mux/input_split stay available as the C reference of the format.
Native claims register their own 'hits' section (owner hits_native) when
the pilot slots arm (arm_pilot_section); 'carry' stays the Lua test carrier.
Per admitted tick r on every client:
1. the multiplexer's hits apply stages every slot's payload in slot order
   (gate.hit_claims_admit; a refusal is identical everywhere: logged as
   HIT_CLAIMS_REFUSED, folded as staged=N/refused=M, not fatal);
2. pilot_frame stages the pilot records (pilot_records[slot]), then resolves
   the claims due (gate.hit_claims_resolve(r)) and appends the HITS_1 row to
   the pilot receipt (declared state);
3. the multiplexer's proposal offers the hits section its budget (hex
   characters; 412 after a pilot section) and gate.hit_claims_proposal(r,
   budget) returns the oldest queued claims that fit; the rest stay queued
   until their observe tick would leave the 8-tick window (then expired).
Protocol 4 (lookahead k, 112bd45d): a blob sealed with tick r-1-k is admitted
at r, so admission reconstructs the observe tick relative to r-1-k (claims
sealed before tick 0 never exist) and the validation bound widens by k ticks
(an incoming claim waits 1+k ticks). Outgoing claims need d >= k+1, which the
pilot module's d >= k+2 already guarantees. Native native_gate_hit_claims
values other than carry/true/false/shooter/victim/first, and native claims
without native_gate_pilot_inputs, are refused before loading.

D. CONFIGURATION

  X4LAN_CONFIG.native_gate_pilot_fire = 'record'   -- or true, 'script'
  X4LAN_CONFIG.native_gate_hit_claims = 'shooter'  -- or true, 'victim', 'first'
Both need native_gate_bullet_cadence = 'every'; firing needs
native_gate_actor_rng_all = true; claims need the wire source (protocol 3)
and world identities. Prediction (native_gate_pilot_predict) is optional for
claims: without it the owner decides at the simulated pose. Sandbox builder:
--fire record|script, --hit-claims shooter|victim|first (with --predict1/2).
Logs: PILOT_FIRE_ARMED, PILOT_FIRE_STATUS (every 600 ticks), PILOT_FIRE_STOP;
HIT_CLAIMS_ARMED, HIT_CLAIMS tick= (rows with outcomes), HIT_CLAIMS_REFUSED,
HIT_CLAIMS_STATUS, HIT_CLAIMS_STOP. Declared state: PFIRE_1 and HITS_1 rows.
