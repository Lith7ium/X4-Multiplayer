X4 MULTIPLAYER — HUMAN ON-FOOT AVATAR TEST
26 October 2026 — INTERNAL {{VERSION}} CANDIDATE

This document starts no game or server. Use one matched dedicated set (server plus
at least two clients; three clients for W06) built from the same commit, a fresh
shared world and fresh client folders. Internal session packages switch avatars on
(walking_avatars=true in the generated config.lua); check that line on every client.
Keep combat, cargo and mission cases out of this run.

Record the case ID, the time, each client's player number, and the station name
and ID code (e.g. ABC-123) where you stand. For each case report PASS / FAIL /
INCONCLUSIVE / NOT RUN with a short description of every client's view. A short clip
helps with jumps and animation. After the run, collect each client's X4 debug log
(procedure in HUMAN-DAMAGE-TEST.txt) and the server log; search for "WALK_".

Useful log lines (clients):
  WALK_MD_READY / WALK_READY          avatars are armed (once after the world loads)
  WALK_SEND sent=N state=S kind=K     this client publishes its own pose (every 5 s)
  WALK_AVATAR slot=pK created         first avatar for player K
  WALK_SHOW / WALK_PLACED slot=pK     player K is drawn (ok=true) and where
  WALK_HIDE / WALK_HIDDEN slot=pK reason=...
        stale (no pose for 1 s), not_on_foot (piloting/spacesuit), context (station or
        ship not found here), elsewhere (another sector), floor (no walkable floor at
        that position here), forget (disconnected for 30 s)
  WALK_DROP reason=...                a received pose was refused (format, slot, order ...)
  WALK_ROOM_MISMATCH                  placed on a different local room than the sender's
  WALK_TRANSFER / WALK_TRANSPORT      elevator (transporter) rides, local and remote
  WALK_TRIGGER                        a local door/terminal trigger (never replicated)

W01 — TWO CLIENTS ON ONE STATION
Both clients dock at the same station (LAN Trading Station Beta first, then any NPC
station from the starting sector) and leave their seats. Client A walks slowly
across the dock area; client B watches. Expected on B: a standing/walking character
named "Player <A's number>" at A's position, facing A's direction, moving smoothly
(no jumps every 0.2 s), feet on the floor. Swap roles. Note any position offset in
metres and whether the walk animation plays or the figure slides.

W02 — LEAVE AND RETURN TO THE SEAT
Client A goes back to the cockpit and takes the pilot seat. Expected on B: A's figure
disappears within about one second (WALK_HIDE reason=not_on_foot); A's ship stays
visible as before. A leaves the seat again: the figure reappears at the ship.

W03 — ELEVATOR / TRANSPORTER
Client A uses the platform elevator (transporter) from the landing pad to the dock
area, and later to another destination (e.g. the trading or bar area if the station
offers one). B stands next to the elevator. Expected on B: A's figure walks into the
elevator, the elevator door/animation plays, and the figure appears at the
destination (WALK_TRANSPORT started ... finished). If B's game has no such room
(interior not generated there), A's figure is hidden with reason=floor rather than
placed somewhere wrong. Record what B sees in both cases and whether the elevator
door moves on B. Doors that open by proximity are local: note whether they open for
A's figure on B.

W04 — UNDOCK, OTHER STATION, OTHER SECTOR
A undocks, flies to a second station in the same sector, docks and walks. B stays.
Expected: A's figure disappears at undock and does not appear at the old station;
when B also docks at the second station, A's figure is there. Repeat with a station
in another sector: while B is in a different sector, the log shows reason=elsewhere
and nothing is drawn.

W05 — DISCONNECT
A closes the game (or loses the connection) while standing on the station. Expected
on B: the figure disappears within about one second (reason=stale) and is forgotten
after 30 s (reason=forget). A rejoins and walks again: the figure returns
("WALK_REMOTE slot=pK new connection" on B).

W06 — THREE CLIENTS
Three clients stand on the same station. Each must see the two others, each with
its own figure and name, moving independently. One client leaves the station:
only that figure disappears on the other two.

W07 — INSIDE A SHARED SHIP (only if the shared ship has a walkable interior)
A walks inside its own docked shared ship (behind the cockpit, if possible). B boards
the same ship. Expected on B: A's figure inside the ship at the right place. With the
supplied S-class ship this case is usually NOT RUN; report it as such.

Report the first failing step and keep all logs. A figure visible in one game is not
proof of shared physics: the avatar is a local NPC that follows the remote pose.
